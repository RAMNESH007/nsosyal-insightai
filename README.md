# NSosyal InsightAI

**NSosyal InsightAI - Explainable, Turkish-native AI trust layer for social platforms - transparent feed ranking (Feed DNA), well-being-aware pacing, and fair creator monetization. Built for TEKNOFEST 2026 NSosyal Innovation Competition**
*Built by Team GarudParadox*

---

## The problem

Social media in Turkey runs on the same playbook everywhere else: engagement-optimized feeds that quietly reward sensational content, moderation systems trained mostly on English that miss the nuances of Turkish, and a creator economy where people have no real idea why they earn what they earn or who might want to sponsor them.

These aren't abstract problems here. Turkey has around 62.3 million social media users — 70.9% of the population — and a national survey found 24.4% of Turkish teenagers meet the criteria for social media addiction. After the February 2023 earthquakes, recycled footage from unrelated disasters spread as if it were live, alongside false donation pages, while other posts genuinely helped coordinate rescue efforts. And Turkish creators deal with low local ad rates, heavy sponsorship dependence, and a tax exemption framework almost nobody actually understands.

## The idea

Instead of bolting on three separate features for three separate problems, NSosyal InsightAI is built around one idea: **one explainable AI engine, pointed in two directions.**

For people scrolling their feed, that engine explains itself — every post carries a plain-language reason it's there, a living map called Feed DNA shows what your feed is actually made of, and a conversational companion named Aya lets you just ask "why am I seeing this" instead of digging through settings.

For the people making content, the same engine turns around and does the same thing for them — a transparent Economic Score, AI-matched brand deals with real percentages and real reasons attached, fair pay that rewards genuine quality instead of engagement bait, and a plain-language guide to Turkey's actual creator tax exemption law.

It's not three products wearing one name. It's one trust layer, viewed from both sides.

## What's actually built here

A Turkish-native content classifier. An explainable ranking engine where every score comes with its reasoning attached. A mode that activates during declared emergencies and specifically counters the kind of misinformation pattern seen after the 2023 earthquake. A full creator economics layer running on a synthetic demo dataset, with 25 passing tests behind the core logic.

## Where things stand

The technical foundation — classification, ranking, the creator economy scoring, the conversational layer — is built, tested, and deployed. What's ahead is the product layer on top of it: real authentication, a persistent database for user preferences, and new interface screens for a couple of features that are implemented on the backend but don't have dedicated screens yet. The AI models themselves are intentionally lightweight for this phase, built to be swapped for trained, production-grade versions later without touching anything else in the system.
